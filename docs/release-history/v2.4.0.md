# Ordo v2.4.0

- Completed tasks disappear immediately from the active Tasks page.
- Expired reminders and past events no longer appear in their active pages.
- Reminder creation now offers one-tap presets: 15 minutes, 30 minutes, 1 hour, 5 hours, and 10 hours.
- “Custom time” remains available for a specific date and time.
- The same quick reminder presets are available from the home-screen widget overlay.
- Completed/past items are kept in storage for future history/archive support.
